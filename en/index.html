[Quest]

Parking App

Conceptualization, integral product design and fullstack development of a mobile app that connects drivers with available parking spots, ensuring their ideal space in seconds.

Year 2022
Type Academic Project
Role UX/UI Designer + Developer
Figma React Native Node.js Express JavaScript
The problem

Why Quest?

Difficulty finding parking

Finding a place to park, whether temporary or permanent, is a daily challenge in the city.

Vehicle overcrowding

Especially in busy neighborhoods, demand exceeds parking supply.

High prices

Parking rentals and rates are expensive, making it difficult to rent more than one.

Scattered listings

Monthly parking rentals are scattered across different platforms.

The solution

A comprehensive platform

Unified platform

A single platform that unifies parking sales and rentals — both fixed and transient — with centralized listing management and fee payment.

Parking network

Generate a network of parking lots for rent.

Subscription system

A subscription system that makes renting more than one parking spot easier, with flexible plans tailored to each user's needs and economic and security benefits.

Quick and simple management

Simple and quick reservation management with specific schedule.

Target audience

Who are we designing for?

Urban drivers

People who drive daily through the city and need to park quickly, safely, and predictably without circling around.

Parking owners

Owners with idle garages or spaces who want to monetize them by listing spots for sale or rent with centralized management.

Frequent drivers

Users who need more than one parking spot per month (work, home, branches) and look for subscription plans with economic benefits.

Occasional drivers

Those who only need a spot for a limited time (events, trips, appointments) and value booking in advance without long commitments.

User journey

User journey

Case 1 — Find and book a transient parking spot

Goal: find and secure a spot for a limited time without circling around.

Open app and search
The user opens Quest, sees the map with available spots near their destination, and applies time and price filters.
  • Geolocation
  • Filters: time, price
  • Real-time listing
Select spot
Reviews photos, address, rate, and availability. Compares nearby options before deciding.
  • Listing detail
  • Photo gallery
  • Hourly/daily rate
Book and pay
Sets duration with the circular selector, pays in the app, and gets instant confirmation with access instructions.
  • Duration selector
  • Integrated payment
  • Instant confirmation
Use and finish
Arrives, parks, and manages the active session with notifications. Rates the experience when finished.
  • Active session + timer
  • Notifications
  • Rating
Case 3 — Rent a parking spot with subscription

Goal: secure a fixed monthly spot with a plan that saves costs.

Browse rentals
Enters the garage marketplace, filters by zone near work or home, and reviews monthly options.
  • Marketplace
  • Zone filter
  • Monthly price
Choose plan
Compares subscription plans by number of spots and economic and security benefits.
  • Flexible plans
  • Plan benefits
  • Cost comparison
Subscribe
Completes registration, links the payment method, and activates the subscription with recurring access to the spot.
  • User registration
  • Payment method
  • Recurring access
Manage from dashboard
Manages bookings, payments, and contracted spots from a single dashboard with history and billing.
  • Single dashboard
  • History and billing
  • Multiple spots
Case 4 — List and monetize a parking spot

Goal: owner lists their idle garage and starts earning income.

Create listing
Registers the spot with photos, location, capacity, available hours, and type (sale or rental).
  • Photos and location
  • Capacity and hours
  • Type: sale/rental
Set rates
Configures hourly, daily, or monthly prices and conditions. Payment is centralized on the platform.
  • Flexible pricing
  • Centralized payment
  • Conditions
Receive bookings
Bookings arrive with notifications. Manages occupancy, access, and requests from the owner dashboard.
  • Notifications
  • Owner dashboard
  • Occupancy control
Collect and scale
Receives income with clear reports. Can add more spots and enable recurring subscriptions.
  • Income reports
  • Multiple spots
  • Subscriptions

Benchmark

Experience benchmark

Uber

Real-time map with immediate availability and reliable ETA: maximum clarity on "how far away?".

Booking flow in a few taps: destination → option → confirmation, frictionless.

Price transparency from the start; the user is never surprised at payment.

Airbnb

Content-rich listings: photos, map, reviews, and filters build trust in host/space.

Two-sided marketplace: guests and hosts with differentiated roles and flows.

Ratings and reviews as core social proof for decisions.

Pedidos Ya

Live order status tracking: sense of control while waiting.

Personalized recommendations based on history and nearby context.

Simple checkout with saved local payment methods.
